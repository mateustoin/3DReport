Where to find the weight and time in your slicer

After slicing the model, these two numbers show up in the print summary, even before you export the G-code.

Bambu Studio and OrcaSlicer

In the right panel, after slicing, you see the filament weight (g) and the estimated print time, just above the export button.

PrusaSlicer

On the "Slice now" tab (Preview), the summary in the bottom right corner shows the filament weight and the total print time.

Cura

In the bottom right corner, after slicing, you see the estimated time and the weight of the filament used.

What each cost means

The same formula as the app, explained line by line.

Material

The filament used in the part, from the weight you entered and the price per kilo.

Energy

The printer's power use during the print time, from the power you entered and the price per kWh.

Failure reserve

A percentage added to the cost to cover prints that fail halfway and have to be redone.

Finishing

A percentage of the material to cover sanding, painting or removing supports after the print.

Your work

The time you spend preparing the file, slicing, taking the part off the bed and packing, at your hourly rate. It goes into the price at face value, without the profit margin on top: your hour is already what you want to earn for it.

This calculator uses the same formula as the 3DReport app, but without printer maintenance, the machine's return on investment and the monthly fixed cost, which depend on your setup. See the full calculation in How the calculation works or in the formula documentation (in Portuguese).
